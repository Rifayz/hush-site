hush privacy policy

hush is a personal video player. This page explains what it does with your Google account data. Last updated 25 October 2026.

What hush accesses in Google Drive

  • Read-only access to your Drive files (drive.readonly): hush lists the folder you choose, reads video and subtitle file names, streams videos to your device, and downloads the ones you ask it to. It never changes, moves or deletes anything in your Drive.
  • Its own hidden app folder (drive.appdata): hush keeps one small file there with your watch progress (which titles you've watched and where you stopped), so your devices stay in step. Other apps and people can't see this folder.

Where your data goes

Everything stays on your own devices and in your own Google Drive. hush has no server, no account system, no analytics and no ads, and it doesn't sell or share your data with anyone.

To show titles, artwork and descriptions, hush looks up movie and show names (taken from file names) on TMDB, and it searches SubDL for subtitles when you ask. Only the title, year and episode numbers are sent; nothing from your Google account.

How Google data is used

hush's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide the features described above, is never transferred to others, and is never used for advertising.

Removing access

Disconnect Google Drive in hush's settings, or remove hush at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Downloads saved to your device stay there until you delete them.
